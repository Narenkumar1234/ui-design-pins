Description

Nestled among the dramatic Bernese Oberland peaks, Lake Brienz is renowned for its surreal turquoise glacial waters and serene mountain panoramas. Glide across mirror-like waters on historic paddle steamers, discover idyllic wooden chalet hamlets in Iseltwald, and hike picturesque trails beside thundering Giessbach Falls. An unforgettable alpine retreat tailored for both slow travel and mountain adventures.
