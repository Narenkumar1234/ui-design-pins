Travel Booking App UI: Roam Soft Glass Light + Dark · Front-End Project Idea

Front-end project idea: Roam, a travel booking and destination explorer app in a soft glassmorphism style. Home with search, category chips and tall photo cards with frosted rating pills, Explore with a big feature card, and a destination detail page with facilities and a Book Now sheet. Pale-sky light mode (#F4F9FF) and deep teal-navy dark mode (#0F1E2E) with one azure accent (#3B8CF5). Build it with HTML, Tailwind CSS and vanilla JavaScript: live search, chip filters, favourites and a dark toggle saved in localStorage. From @code.in.30s, comment ROAM on Instagram for the HTML/CSS kit.
