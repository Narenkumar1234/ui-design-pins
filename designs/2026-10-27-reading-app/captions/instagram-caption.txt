A reading tracker that feels like a good book 📚🟠

Build this → Folio, a minimal book reading tracker

1️⃣ Home: a 12-day reading streak with last-7-days bars, the book you're on with progress, a daily goal ring and a + Log sheet (pages + minutes)
2️⃣ Book detail: big cover, rating · pages · genre pills, an Add to shelf pill, your 5-star rating and a sticky orange Continue reading button
3️⃣ Library: search, status tabs (Reading · Want · Finished · On shelf) and genre chips
4️⃣ Reader: paged text, tap a paragraph to highlight it, Aa sheet for size + line height, swipe or arrow keys to turn pages
5️⃣ Profile: stat tiles (books, pages, hours, longest streak), a yearly goal, your shelf and favourite genres
6️⃣ Monochrome light mode + a near-black dark mode, same orange accent

🎓 Student project idea: make it full-stack. Next.js front end, book search through the free Open Library API, Postgres + Prisma for books, reading sessions and highlights, auth, streaks calculated from your sessions and a nightly cron that sends "keep your streak" reminders. Stretch: follow friends, public shelves, Goodreads CSV import, a yearly "wrapped" page
