Your job hunt deserves better than a messy spreadsheet 📋🚀

Build this → Orbit, a job application tracker dashboard

1️⃣ Dashboard: bento stats (applied, interviews, offers, response rate), a weekly applications chart (4 / 8 weeks) with a goal line and tooltips, upcoming interviews and a pipeline bar
2️⃣ Board: drag and drop cards across Wishlist → Applied → Interview → Offer → Rejected, undo, keyboard moves, filters, search and sort
3️⃣ Application page: status timeline, autosaving notes, contacts, and an "add to calendar" .ics button
4️⃣ Interviews: a month calendar with arrow-key navigation, a schedule form and .ics invites
5️⃣ Settings: weekly goal, ₹/$ currency, interview reminders, CSV import/export and reset
6️⃣ Responsive mobile view with stage tabs + a forest/lime dark mode

🎓 Student project idea: make it full-stack. Next.js/React front end, Node/Express or Next API routes, Postgres + Prisma for applications and stages, auth (NextAuth / Clerk), cron jobs that email interview and follow-up reminders, resume uploads to S3, and an analytics page (response rate, time to first reply, funnel by stage). Stretch: a Chrome extension that saves a job post in one click
