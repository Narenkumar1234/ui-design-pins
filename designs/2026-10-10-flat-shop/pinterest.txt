DESCRIPTION (use for both pins):
Here's a flat-design e-commerce app to build as a front-end project. It has a home screen with search, category chips and a featured product grid; a product detail page with size and color pickers and a sticky Add to Cart bar; and a cart with qty steppers, a free-shipping banner, a coupon field and checkout. Build it with HTML, CSS and JavaScript (or React) plus Tailwind, fetch products from the Fake Store API, and keep the cart and totals in localStorage. Follow @code.in.30s for more UI ideas you can build. #flatdesign #uidesign #frontenddevelopment #ecommercedesign #tailwindcss #codingprojects
