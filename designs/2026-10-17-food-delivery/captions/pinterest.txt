Food Delivery App UI: Ember Cream + Espresso Dark · Front-End Project Idea

Front-end project idea: Ember, a warm, editorial food delivery app. Home with a serif hero, overhead bowl photo, search, round category chips, a promo card and "Popular near you" cards; a dish detail page with an extras checklist and live total; and a cart with promo code, order summary and Place order. Cream light mode (#FBF4EC) and warm espresso dark mode (#1B1411) with one terracotta accent (#E8622C), Newsreader + DM Sans. Build it with HTML, Tailwind CSS and vanilla JavaScript (cart and dark mode in localStorage), then go full-stack with React/Next.js, Node, MongoDB, Stripe test mode and Socket.IO order tracking. From @code.in.30s, comment EMBER on Instagram for the HTML/CSS kit.
