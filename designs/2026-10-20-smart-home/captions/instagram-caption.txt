Smart home apps can feel this warm 🏡🧡

Build this → Hearth, a charcoal + orange smart home control app

1️⃣ Welcome: full-bleed cozy living-room photo, "Your home, on one tap", stepper dots and a slide-to-start "Get Started" pill
2️⃣ Home: "Hi, Aarav", pill search, room chips (All · Living room · Bedroom · Kitchen), device cards with real toggles and ONE full-orange active card
3️⃣ Room: a 270° thermostat dial you can drag, Cool / Heat / Dry / Fan modes, fan speed, schedule, plus a lights tab with a brightness slider and 4 colour temperatures
4️⃣ Energy: Day / Week / Month bar chart (tap a bar for kWh), top devices, one-tap scenes like Movie night and Away
5️⃣ Floating rounded nav with a centred orange + button that adds a new device
6️⃣ Charcoal dark (#1A1A1A) as the hero + a warm off-white light mode (#F5F2EE), same orange

🎓 Student project idea: make it a full-stack smart home app. Next.js/React front end, Node + Express (or Next API routes), MongoDB or Postgres for homes, rooms, devices and energy readings, WebSockets + MQTT for live device state (simulate an ESP32!), JWT auth with family members, and usage charts
