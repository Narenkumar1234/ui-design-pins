Smart Home App UI: Hearth Charcoal + Orange, Light + Dark · Front-End Project Idea

Front-end project idea: Hearth, a smart home control mobile app UI. A welcome screen with a cozy living-room photo and a slide-to-start pill; a home dashboard with pill search, room filter chips and device cards with toggles (one full-orange active card); a room screen with a draggable thermostat dial, AC modes, fan speed, a light brightness slider and colour temperatures; and an energy screen with a day / week / month usage chart, top devices and one-tap scenes. Charcoal dark mode (#1A1A1A, cards #2A2A2A) and warm off-white light mode (#F5F2EE) with one orange accent (#F2762E), floating bottom nav with an orange + button, Outfit font. Build it with HTML, Tailwind CSS and vanilla JavaScript (device state, scenes and dark mode in localStorage), then go full-stack with Next.js/React, Node + Express, MongoDB or Postgres, WebSockets/MQTT for live device state, JWT auth and energy charts. A smart home / IoT dashboard UI kit from @code.in.30s, comment HEARTH on Instagram for the HTML/CSS kit.
