E-learning App UI: Lumo Pastel 3D Clay, Light + Dark · Front-End Project Idea

Front-end project idea: Lumo, a cute pastel 3D clay e-learning / online course app. A home screen with a floating clay lightbulb mascot, Continue pill, progress card and streak; an Explore page with filter pills and a staggered grid of pastel subject cards; a lesson player with play, seek, speed, lessons, notes and a quiz; and a My learning page with a streak tracker, weekly goal chart and badges. Soft sky-blue light mode (#F4F8FF) and deep navy dark mode (#121A2B) with a periwinkle accent (#6FA3F0) and peach, cream and lavender tiles, Fredoka + Nunito. Build it with HTML, Tailwind CSS and vanilla JavaScript (progress, streak and dark mode in localStorage), then go full-stack with React/Next.js, Node, MongoDB, JWT auth and Cloudinary video. From @code.in.30s, comment LUMO on Instagram for the HTML/CSS kit.
