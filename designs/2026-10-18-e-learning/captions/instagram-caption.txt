Study apps can be this cute 📚✨

Build this → Lumo, a pastel 3D clay e-learning app

1️⃣ Home: greeting + avatar, a floating clay lightbulb mascot, "Learn something new today", one big Continue pill, progress card with a 6-day streak, pastel "Today's picks" tiles
2️⃣ Explore: "What do you want to learn today?", filter pills (All · Popular · New · Free), a staggered 2-column grid of pastel subject cards with 3D clay icons, live search
3️⃣ Lesson player: play / seek / 1.5x speed, Lessons · Notes · Quiz tabs, done ticks on finished lessons, one Next lesson pill
4️⃣ My learning: streak tracker, weekly minutes chart with a daily-goal line, badges
5️⃣ Light (sky #F4F8FF) + deep navy dark (#121A2B, not black), same periwinkle accent

🎓 Student project idea: turn it into a full-stack e-learning app with React/Next.js + Node/Express + MongoDB: JWT auth, course + lesson CRUD, video lessons on Cloudinary, saved progress, streaks and quiz scores per user
