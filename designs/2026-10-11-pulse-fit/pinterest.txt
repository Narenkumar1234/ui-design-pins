## Description
Front-end project idea: Pulse Fit — a neo-brutalist workout tracker with Today, Workout and Progress screens. Thick 3–4px outlines, hard offset shadows, lime + pink accents. Build it with HTML, Tailwind CSS and vanilla JS. Streaks, rest timer and dark mode included. From @code.in.30s — comment PULSE on Instagram for the kit.
