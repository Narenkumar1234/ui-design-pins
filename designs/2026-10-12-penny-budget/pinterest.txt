## Description
Front-end project idea: Penny, a minimalist personal finance app with Overview, Add Expense and Insights screens. Lots of whitespace, 1px hairline borders, large light numerals and only two accents (green #1F7A5A for income, terracotta #C2553A for expenses). Build it with HTML, Tailwind CSS and vanilla JavaScript: save transactions and a monthly budget in localStorage, format ₹ with Intl.NumberFormat, and draw a CSS-only bar chart. Light and dark mode included. From @code.in.30s, comment PENNY on Instagram for the HTML/CSS kit.
