Build this → Penny, a minimalist expense tracker
Lots of whitespace, 1px hairlines, big light numerals and just two accent colors.

📱 3 screens to ship:
1️⃣ Overview: monthly balance, income vs spent, budget progress line, recent transactions
2️⃣ Add expense: big amount display, custom keypad, category chips, note/date/account
3️⃣ Insights: Week / Month / Year toggle, slim bar chart, spending by category

☀️ Light: #FAFAF7 bg · #FFFFFF cards · #ECEAE4 hairlines · #17181C ink · #6E6F76 muted · #1F7A5A green · #C2553A terracotta
🌙 Dark: #0F1012 bg · #17181B cards · #26282D hairlines · #EDEDEA ink · #8E9097 muted · #4FC59A green · #E07A5F terracotta

🛠 Stack: HTML · Tailwind CSS (dark: variants) · vanilla JS · localStorage for transactions + budget · Intl.NumberFormat for ₹ · CSS-only bar chart
Fonts: Manrope + Inter · Screens designed with Google Stitch
